Rates on the untracked products have not been read, so this ranks coverage, not pricing.

How to read it

  • The first four columns are the products that behave like a fixed-term savings product: a single premium earns a crediting rate fixed for a set period. Within those columns, two products compete when they share a currency and a guarantee term, whatever their legal form.
  • Guarantee terms are recorded only for the eight tracked products. For the rest, the term has to be read from each product or rate page before matching is reliable.
  • "Whole life, to classify" holds products the research recorded only as whole life. Many will move into the rate-locked or periodic-payout columns once their formal policy names are read.
  • Dashed chips are placed by name or by a distributor page that did not state the type or premium mode. Nissay Wealth is placed from distributor pages only, because its own site could not be read.
  • Deduplication is done for Dai-ichi Frontier (from its rate hub: names listed together under one policy form and one rate page), MetLife (from its rate list) and Credit Agricole. PGF, Sumitomo and Meiji Yasuda are folded from their own rate pages. Mitsui Sumitomo Primary, T&D Financial and Manulife reuse one policy form under several names but publish a separate rate page for each, so those stay as separate products inside a bracketed family. Nissay Wealth could not be checked, because its site blocks access; its names are listed as found.
  • Gibraltar is under a regulator's suspension order from 13 October 2026 to 31 January 2027; its own release says agency sales are outside the order.
